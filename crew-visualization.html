WHAT HAPPENS

A milestone in motion

    Frame the work

    Vision and roadmap give the Foreman its context.

  2. 02
    Delegate an assignment

    A focused brief is handed to the right specialist.

  3. 03
    Produce evidence

    The specialist changes code, tests, or architecture and logs the outcome.

  4. 04
    Audit the result

    The Auditor passes or returns the assignment for correction.

    Advance deliberately

    Only a verified assignment moves the roadmap forward.
